What I did in the code

  1. 1
    Searched openFDA shortages for the drug's exact name: three records, from Ritedose, Nephron and Rising, all at 2.5 mg/.5 mL.
  2. 2
    Searched the NDC Directory for every manufacturer of the same generic name and dosage form: 63 matches.
  3. 3
    Kept only products at exactly the shortage strength with a single ingredient, then split them into the three shortage manufacturers and one substitute candidate, Chartwell RX.
  4. 4
    Looked up each manufacturer's package codes in the Medicaid national totals (State = XX) instead of adding up states by hand.
  5. 5
    Added the two reporting channels for each quarter and flagged quarters with suppressed rows.
