Overall score

A small gain, in one place

ModelMAE (units)MAPE

The gain looks modest because it sits in a single quarter. It is a targeted, explainable improvement, not a general gain in accuracy.

One quarter changes, and its error falls from 28.2% to 18.6%. Step 06 tests whether that is more than luck.

The flagged quarter was picked from the same series being forecast, which is why Step 06 also tries every other candidate quarter.
