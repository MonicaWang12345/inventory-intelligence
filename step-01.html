What I did in the code

  1. 1
    Counted the openFDA shortage records and broke them down by status and dosage form, using the API's count queries.
  2. 2
    Compared the date fields for the four manufacturers' NDC records, at product level and at package level.
  3. 3
    Downloaded four years of Medicaid utilization files and combined them into one table of 19.8 million rows. Drug codes are kept as text so leading zeros survive.
  4. 4
    Profiled every column: missing values, unique values and sample values.
  5. 5
    Checked that the missing numbers line up exactly with the suppression flag, that no rows are duplicated, and that the smallest visible prescription count is 11.
  6. 6
    Split utilization by reporting channel, measured suppression at the national level, and confirmed every state reports every quarter.
